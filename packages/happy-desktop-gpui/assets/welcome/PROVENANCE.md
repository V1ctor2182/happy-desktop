# Native welcome artwork provenance

`scene-multiplayer.png`, `scene-models.png`, `scene-adaptable.png`, and
`scene-security.png` are original deterministic Happy GPUI artwork generated by
`scripts/build-welcome-art.py` for the native first-run deck. They do not copy
another feature's raster art.

The two logo and two sky files are byte-for-byte copies of the authoritative
assets used by this same Happy welcome feature in `happy-desktop-ui`. The
generator pins their SHA-256 identities and fails if those sources drift.

## Original scene masters

`masters/scene-*.png` are the retained deterministic 640 × 640 source canvases.
The top-level `scene-*.png` files are 320 × 320 Retina derivatives rendered in the native 160 px art slot. `build-welcome-art.py` normalizes each source by its alpha-weighted centroid before deriving the packaged asset.
